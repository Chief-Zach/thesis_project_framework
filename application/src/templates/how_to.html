How To

Instructions

Instructions will appear here for each level. The root of each level is at
/games/{game name}, such as game. Buttons such as the ones below will appear for each level, and allow you to simulate web requests and open new pages

Hints

Hints will appear here for each level after the instructions. Hints are available after the "frontend" button is clicked, and you are navigated to the levels page. Hints (will be) generated with the assistance of a LLM, and the contents of your past queries will be passed to allow for more accurate context

 

Verification

Each level has a verification endpoint at /games/{game name}/verify that grants you access to the next level. What is required to be passed to this endpoint varies by level, but it will always include the password, cookie, hash, or whatever other sensitive information you have obtained.

Feedback

Feedback on this project is greatly appreciated! You can submit your feedback on things such as the hint quality, level content, or whatever else you feel is appropriate. The button below will lead you to a Google Form.
